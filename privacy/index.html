Privacy Policy

VINCI Lab, a brand of Vinci Automação (Rio de Janeiro, Brazil) · Felyx, Leda and Leo assistants and the Felyx Google Home integration · last updated November 21, 2026

VINCI Lab (Vinci Automação) designs and installs smart home automation systems — lighting, climate, shades and curtains, audio/video and scenes — and provides the Felyx, Leda and Leo home assistants that let customers control their home by voice and app. This policy explains what data we process when you use these assistants, the subscription pages at vinciautomacao.com and the Felyx integration with Google Home. It follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018).

Data controller: VINCI Lab / Vinci Automação, Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Privacy contact: Leandro Portella, suporte@vinciautomacao.com (also palanteer.ai@gmail.com).

Data we process

  • Account and subscription: name, email and mobile number you provide, used to create your access, bill the subscription and give support.
  • Payment: payments are processed by Mercado Pago. We never receive or store card numbers; we only receive the payment confirmation and subscription status.
  • Sign-in: passwords and codes are stored only as cryptographic hashes; sessions use random identifiers.
  • Voice and conversation: when you start a voice conversation, audio is streamed in real time to Google Gemini to recognize your speech and generate the reply. We do not record audio. Conversation text and preferences you ask the assistant to remember are kept to maintain context until you ask us to delete them.
  • Home: names of scenes, rooms and devices in your home, their state, and the commands you give, so we can carry out your requests.

Google Home

If you link your account to Google Home, Google receives the list of compatible devices and scenes in your home (lights, outlets, switches, thermostats, blinds, curtains and scenes) and their state (on/off, temperature, open percentage), and sends us the commands you give through Google. We use this data only to execute your commands and report device state back to Google. Locks, gates, alarms and cameras are never shared with Google. You can unlink at any time in the Google Home app; when you unlink, we delete the Google access credentials.

Third-party services

We use Google (Gemini, Google Home, Google Apps Script/Sheets), Mercado Pago (payments), Meta WhatsApp Business (notifications and access codes), GitHub Pages (web hosting) and our own server in a European data center (Njalla). Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

What we do not do

We do not show ads or use advertising tracking. We do not sell data. We do not use your home data to train AI models.

Retention and security

Account data is kept while your subscription is active and deleted within 30 days after you close your account, except where the law requires us to keep billing records. All communication is encrypted (HTTPS/WSS). Each customer can see and control only their own home.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, portability or deletion of your data, or closure of your account, by emailing suporte@vinciautomacao.com. We reply within 15 days. Our apps are not intended for children under 13.
